3 · What We Did Well · What to Improve
✅ What's Working — May vs Mar & Apr
  • Affiliate Live GMV breakthrough: ฿2.10M in May — +39.6% vs Apr and +44.8% vs Mar Mega. BAU May outperformed a Mega Month on aff live for the first time.
  • Total Aff GMV at record ฿4.28M: +21% MoM (Apr) and +26.8% vs Mar — channel scaling in a non-event month is the strongest signal of underlying growth.
  • Video output & sales efficiency recovered: 1,946 videos published (+35% vs Apr), daily videos with sales hit 60.9 — new all-time high, above Mar's 54.2 and well above Apr's 46.5.
  • AOV recovered to ฿528: above both Apr (฿510) and Mar (฿524) — creators closing higher-basket orders in May, suggesting better SKU-audience match post re-brief.
  • Commission ROI improved: blended rate back to 6.3% (same as Mar, down from Apr's 6.6%) while GMV jumped +21% — lower cost per baht of affiliate revenue.
  • Creator content volume at high: 138 creators posting/day (vs 134 Mar, 128 Apr) — creator base is growing and re-activating heading into 6.6 Mega Month.
⚠️ What to Improve — May vs Mar & Apr
  • Refund rate: 3rd consecutive rise — now 13.2%: Mar 11.5% → Apr 12.2% → May 13.2%. At this trajectory, breaches the 15% threshold in Jun Mega. Root cause: high-AOV bundles (Bio C ×2) pushed without purchase-intent qualification in briefs.
  • Video GPM still −10% vs Mar: May ฿147 vs Mar ฿164 (Apr was ฿55 — partially recovered). View volumes normalised post-viral Apr, but GMV-per-view has not fully returned. Re-brief on close-oriented content required pre-6.6.
  • Daily Lives with sales stagnant at 11.7: barely above Apr (11.6), still below Mar (12.4) — despite stream count recovering to 2,479. More streams are not generating more effective sessions; session mechanics need sharpening.
  • Creators with sales (90/day) below Mar peak (91): creator count posting is a new high (138) but conversion from posted → sales declined. Gap = creators posting without a qualified audience or relevant SKU.
  • Samples-to-activation gap persists: 395 shipped vs Mar 410 — new SKUs (Zinc A Chelate, Vitamin E) still under-indexed in creator content. No measurable GMV contribution from these SKUs despite sample push since Mar.
📋 Priority Actions Before 6.6 Mega Month
🎬 VIDEO BRIEF
Re-brief all Tier A/B video creators: first 3 sec must include product benefit + offer hook. GPM floor = ฿300. Remove creators below ฿100 GPM from collab plan.
📡 LIVE ACTIVATION
Recruit 15–20 new mid-tier live creators (10K–100K followers). Target: +20% active live streams with sales. Provide exclusive in-session GMV mechanics for 6.6.
🛒 COLLAB PUSH
Add Zinc A Chelate + Vitamin E + Bio Calcium+D3 to target collaboration with GMV incentive tiers. These 3 SKUs are under-indexed in affiliate vs. self-sell.
