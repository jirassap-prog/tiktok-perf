3 · What We Did Well · What to Improve
✅ What's Working
  • Affiliate Live GMV sustained: ฿1.2–1.5M/month consistently across all 5 months — stable floor even in low-traffic months
  • LIVE streams with sales efficiency: avg 14.2 LIVE sessions with sales per day — high activation rate among active live creators
  • Creator AOV ฿515 above product card baseline (฿447) — live and video creators are driving higher basket size, especially in Payday and DD periods
  • Commission rate at 6.6% — well within ROI-positive range (benchmark: <10% for health/wellness TikTok)
  • Mar Mega Month uplift: affiliate live and video both spiked on 3.3 and category days — creators responding to campaign mechanics
⚠️ What to Improve
  • Video GPM collapsed: Jan ฿848 → Apr ฿53 — views grew 26× but GMV only 1.6×. Content briefs are not aligned to purchase intent. Re-brief required before 6.6
  • Apr live streams down 23% from Jan (3,125→2,405) while views up — fewer but larger streams. Losing long-tail creator volume; need to activate mid-tier creators
  • Daily creators with sales plateau: 82→88 range shows no growth in active seller base. Target: 100+ daily creators with sales by Jun Mega Month
  • Refund rate creeping up in Apr: linked to broader product mismatch — affiliates promoting high-AOV SKUs (Bio C 1000mg ×2) without qualifying audience
  • Avg daily products in collaborations: low collaboration uptake on new SKUs (Zinc, Vitamin E). Need to push these into target collab plans with GMV incentives
📋 Priority Actions Before 6.6 Mega Month
🎬 VIDEO BRIEF
Re-brief all Tier A/B video creators: first 3 sec must include product benefit + offer hook. GPM floor = ฿300. Remove creators below ฿100 GPM from collab plan.
📡 LIVE ACTIVATION
Recruit 15–20 new mid-tier live creators (10K–100K followers). Target: +20% active live streams with sales. Provide exclusive in-session GMV mechanics for 6.6.
🛒 COLLAB PUSH
Add Zinc A Chelate + Vitamin E + Bio Calcium+D3 to target collaboration with GMV incentive tiers. These 3 SKUs are under-indexed in affiliate vs. self-sell.
